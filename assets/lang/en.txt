# ---- training stage, hangar page, mouse / gamepad settings, synergies (this build)
HINT DEAD ZONE	Tilts of the stick smaller than this count as not moving. High: no drift. Low: more sensitive.
HINT MOUSE BUTTON	ENTER, then press the mouse button. A button that is taken swaps places with its action. ESC cancels.
HINT PAD BUTTON	ENTER, then press the button on the pad. A button that is taken swaps places with its action. ESC cancels.
HINT PAD EXTRA	ON: RT shoots, LB switches, RB fires the beam, B and the stick click are the tactic (until the button is bound to another action).
HINT RESET MOUSE	Puts every mouse button back to its default.
HINT RESET PAD	Puts the pad buttons, the vibration and the dead zone back to their defaults.
HINT VIBRATION	How hard the pad shakes on hits and explosions. 0 turns it off. A test pulse comes as you change it.
MENUS ALWAYS USE ARROWS ENTER ESC	Menus always use the arrow keys, ENTER and ESC.
MENUS ALWAYS USE LEFT RIGHT CLICK	Menus always use the left and the right mouse button.
MENUS ALWAYS USE A B START	Menus always use A, B and START.
